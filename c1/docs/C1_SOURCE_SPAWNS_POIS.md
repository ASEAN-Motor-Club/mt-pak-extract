## Coordinate frames

| frame | rule |
|---|---|
| `bng_m` | BeamNG level metres. X east, Y north, Z up. Every `position` in the level JSON. |
| `ue_cm` | Motor Town / Unreal: `(x*100, -y*100, z*100)` — metres→cm with **Y negated**. Matches the level actor transforms (`set_transforms.py`: `loc = (org[0]*100, -org[1]*100, 0)`). |

Two independent checks that the Y sign is right:
- the shipped port PlayerStart `(169915, -692979, 84352)` cm decodes to source `(1699.15, 6929.79)` m, which sits **0.0 m** from the authored C1 loop centreline (`c1_road_points.json`), i.e. exactly on the road;
- `c3_13.obj` bbox is x 1463–1991 m / y 6557–7029 m and that spawn falls inside it.

`dist_to_c1_loop_m` is measured to the **shipped** 14 km loop spline; `dist_to_source_road_m` to the **source's own 305 DecalRoad** network. A POI can be on a real source road and still be outside the port (that is the case for most of them).

## Port equivalents (C1Full)

Every POI is mapped into the **shipped full port**: `zzz_AMC_C1Full_v26_P.pak` (554 cell meshes). World position is the same transform throughout — `ue_cm = (x*100, -y*100, z*100); cell actor at (origin_m*100, -origin_m_y*100, 0) carrying local-space vertices, so world = actor + local`.

Verified against the ported geometry, not just the arithmetic: for each POI the nearest vertex of its containing cell mesh is measured in XY and Z (`deck test` = within 25 m XY and |dz| ≤ 5 m).

- 144 POIs over 75 cells
- 144 of 144 land in a cell whose mesh **ships in the pak** (0 missing)
- 92 of 144 pass the deck test — ported geometry is physically at that XY and height

## What this means for the port

- The shipped corridor is a **subset**: 14 km of loop, 74 cells. Source spawns, garages and most parking are on roads the port does not carry, so "the map has spawns" and "the port has spawns" are different sets and must not be conflated.
- Inside the corridor the source gives you 36 usable anchors — 30 tunnel portals and 6 speed traps — and each sits within ~42 m of the shipped loop spline and within ~28 m of a real source road, so they are directly placeable.
- Mesh/actor transform convention is `(x, -y, z) * 100`; the road-spline authoring JSON is the opposite (`+y`). Mixing the two silently mirrors half the map — check every new placement against the loop spline (expect ≤ ~5 m) before committing it.
- For spawns and garages inside the port, do not invent coordinates from this table: pick a corridor cell, offset laterally off the carriageway, and snap to the cell top surface (`PLAN-garage.md` procedure). Use the source points here only as the *layout* reference (pairing, spacing, entrance orientation).
- **On C1Full the substitution problem disappears.** All 144 source POIs land in cells that ship in the pak, so the source coordinate *is* the placement — no re-siting, no derived offset. Add spawns/garages at these world positions and the deck test above already tells you whether the point sits on geometry (`dz ≈ 0`) or on a structure above/below it.
